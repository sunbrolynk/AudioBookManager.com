---
title: Phone use
description: How ABM behaves on screens narrower than 768px.
---

On screens under 768px wide ABM switches to phone layouts. Wider screens keep the desktop layouts.

## Library lists

- Each book is one row: cover, title, author and narrator, a few small chips, a status dot and the rating.
- Tap the row to open the book. Everything else is behind the **...** button, which opens an actions sheet.
- In selection mode, tapping a row selects it.

## Sort, filter and view

A bar under the header holds the Sort, Filter and view controls (List, Poster, Table) and rows per page. The Filter shows purple while one is active. Totals and the status legend sit under the list.

Poster size sets the columns: small is 3, medium is 2, large is 1.

## Settings

Settings is a grouped list: Library, Sources and App. Each row is a link with the page's name and what it is for.

Download clients and indexers are full-width cards. Add is the plus button in the top bar.

## Import Workbench

- A summary card shows how many folders are ready. Tap it to open the legend and filter the list.
- Hold a row to join rows. Split is in the folder details.
- Tap a row for its folder details. The **...** button opens the actions sheet: select all ready, deselect unmatched, join rows, sort and rescan.
- The bottom bar shows how many are selected and the Add button. It says why Add is blocked.
