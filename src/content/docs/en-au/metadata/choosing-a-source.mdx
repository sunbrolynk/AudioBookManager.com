**Settings > Metadata > Source** has a **Metadata Source** setting with three choices. Until you choose, it is Built-in.

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| Built-in (default) | Libex runs inside ABM. It needs an outbound connection chosen first. |
| <span class="nowrap">Hosted ([LibexDB.com](https://libexdb.com) <a href="https://github.com/LibexHQ/Libex" aria-label="Libex on GitHub"><Icon name="github" size="1em" class="inline-icon" /></a>)</span> | ABM asks the public Libex instance. |
| Self-hosted | ABM asks a Libex server you point it at. Enter its **Server URL**, for example `http://192.168.1.20:8000`. |

**Test** checks the selected source without saving. **Status** says whether it is ready. When you pick a different source, **Status** shows "Not in use yet - save to switch" until you save.

## Built-in and the outbound connection

Built-in talks to Audible from your machine, so it uses **Settings > General > Proxy & VPN**. That page decides the route: a proxy, the built-in WireGuard tunnel, your own VPN container, or a direct connection you acknowledge. With none chosen, automated lookups are blocked and say so. A chosen route that is down blocks lookups too. It never falls back to your own address. Direct shows a warning that Audible sees your IP address and the books ABM looks up.

Every source sends its lookups through the connection chosen under **Settings > Network** and never falls back to another route. A self-hosted server on your LAN, and the covers it serves, are reached directly.

## Hosted and self-hosted

With these two, ABM asks a Libex server and the server talks to Audible. A self-hosted URL is checked before it is saved, and an empty or unsafe one is refused. A server that is down is reported as the metadata source being unavailable, with its name.

## When the source can't be reached

If the source is unreachable or refuses ABM, a refresh, import or sync over many items stops instead of failing every book, and a single timeout only skips that item. You get one notification per kind of outage. Error messages name the source and the real reason, for example that the VPN is down.

When Libex asks ABM to slow down, a search you are waiting on gives up after about 10 seconds, and a background task waits up to 2 minutes.

## Which to pick

- Want it to just work and do not mind Audible seeing your address: Built-in with a direct connection, or Hosted.
- Care about not exposing your IP to Audible: Built-in through a proxy, the built-in WireGuard tunnel or a separate VPN container or service. Or Hosted, where the Libex server talks to Audible instead of you.
- Already run a Libex server: Self-hosted.
