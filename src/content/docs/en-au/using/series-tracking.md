---
title: Series tracking
description: Following a series so new books arrive as wanted.
---

A series in ABM is a catalogue: every book Libex knows in it, with the ones you own marked. It exists whether or not you own anything in it.

## Monitor New Books

Each series and author has a **Monitor New Books** choice. When a refresh finds a book that was not in the catalogue before, this decides whether it is added as wanted. A series you add yourself defaults to All. An author defaults to None. The choice is saved on the series or author, and refreshes apply it.

## Filling gaps

Open a series and add its missing books. They enter as wanted with the profile and root folder you choose, and ABM searches for them like any other wanted book.

## Status you see

The Series list shows owned, wanted and missing-file counts. A downloaded book with no files on disk counts as Missing Files, not owned.

## Gotchas

- A series you added by hand stays, even with no books, until you remove it.
- Box sets with a range position such as "1-7" sort after books with a real number when ABM has to pick a primary series.
- Deleting a series removes its catalogue but never the books: the delete is refused while books remain.
