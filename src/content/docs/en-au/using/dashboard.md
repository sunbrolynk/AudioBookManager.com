The **Dashboard** is the first page ABM opens. It sums up your library and what needs attention, and each heading and figure opens the page behind it.

## What it shows

- **Your library** gives the totals: books, hours of audio, size on disk, authors, series and missing books. The Books figure also says how many have files and how many are missing, and the Series figure says how many series are complete and how many are one book short.
- **New in your library** has two tabs. **Imported** shows the books whose files came in most recently. **Added as wanted** shows the newest books on the [Wanted > Missing](/en-au/using/wanted/) list. **Library Import** and **Add New** sit right below.
- **Coming soon** lists releases from your authors and series over the next 14 days, today included. It draws on the same books as the [Calendar](/en-au/using/calendar/), so only monitored books count.
- **Missing** shows monitored books you don't have yet, five at a time, each with its own **Search** button. **Search all wanted** queues a search for every one of them after you confirm.
- **Series completion** counts your series that are complete, one book short, or missing more. Each count opens the Series page with that filter. The series that are one book short are listed with a **Search missing** button, and **Formats** links to the books waiting for a better format (**Wanted > Cutoff Unmet**).

## On a phone

The totals sit in a grid, with **New**, **Wanted** and **Coming** tabs below it and a short summary of series completion and your most-heard narrator. See [Phone use](/en-au/phone/).
