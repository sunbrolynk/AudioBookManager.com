title: Calendar
description: Upcoming releases and an iCal feed.
---

**Calendar** shows release dates of the books ABM knows about, coloured by status. Views are month, week, forecast, day and agenda. Week start and date formats come from [UI settings](/en-gb/settings/ui/).

## Options

The **Calendar Options** button offers: collapse multiple books releasing on the same day, colour the whole event with the status colour, and show author and series information.

You can also build a custom filter for the calendar. See [Custom filters](/en-gb/using/library/#custom-filters).

## iCal feed

Subscribe from a calendar app with the feed URL (copy it, or use **Subscribe in Calendar App**). Options:

- **Include Unmonitored**: include unmonitored books in the feed.
- **Show as All-Day Events**: events are all-day.
- Tags: the feed then contains only books with at least one matching tag.

The feed is served from ABM, so a calendar app outside your network needs a way to reach it and to authenticate. See [Security and sign-in](/en-gb/settings/security/).
