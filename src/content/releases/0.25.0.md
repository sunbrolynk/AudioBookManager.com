### Changed

- **An import session reports a match the library already owns as in the library.** A folder matched to a book that is already in your library used to come back with the status `matched`. Its status is now `in_library`, and the match says which library book it is and whether that book has files. Recent Import Sessions counts every row once: a row waiting for you to choose is pending, and a row in the library is matched.

### Fixed

- **Metadata lookups recover after a proxy drops a connection.** If a proxy connection to Libex failed partway through setup, metadata lookups could keep timing out until you restarted ABM. The lookup that hits the failure still reports an error, but the next one starts with a fresh connection.

- **Joining rows in Library Import no longer keeps the first row's match.** A joined row used to keep the first row's match as though it had been checked against every file. Its match now goes back under Needs a look, unticked, until you confirm it, and its runtime comes from the joined rows.

- **"Delete these files, keep the library copy" can't delete your only copy.** Before deleting, ABM now checks that your library copy still exists and that the folder isn't the library book's own folder.

- **Library Import accepts a match only when the files confirm it.** A suggestion used to be accepted on its score alone, so a short story could be matched to another book of its series and a lone weak result was taken as the answer. A match is now accepted only when the files' own ASIN finds it, or its runtime is within three minutes of the files and its series position or title agrees. Anything else is still suggested, but the row waits under Needs a look, starts unticked, and Select All leaves it alone until you confirm it or pick another match. The search also looks further, including the author's other books, ranks a book at the wrong series position well below the right one, and tries an ASIN mentioned in the files' tags on every lookup.

- **A book in several files that shares its folder with other books is one row in Library Import.** Loose parts in an author folder, or in the folder you scan, used to come up as one row per file. Files that name the same book and are numbered as its parts are now one row holding every part. Files that differ by a book or volume number stay separate books.

- **Tags written as "Title: Series, Book N" are read as a title in a series.** Audible-style album tags such as "Artificial Condition: The Murderbot Diaries, Book 2" used to give a series named after the whole text. The title, the series and the position are now read apart, a numbered or dated folder no longer invents a series out of its title, and "(Unabridged)" is dropped from a multi-file book's title.

### Security

- **Library Import's lookup and scan refuse text no real tag holds.** `POST /api/library-import/retry-match` takes at most 100 rows a request, and a row with more than 500 characters in a field (its author names counted together) comes back as an error row without being searched. A scan ignores any text tag longer than 500 characters instead of reading a title or series out of it.
